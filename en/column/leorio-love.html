Why Leorio Paradinight Is My All-Time Favorite Character in Hunter x Hunter

"When you are young, you idolize lightning assassins. Once you grow up, you realize the loud teenager in a sharp suit carrying a medical dream is the bravest person in the room."
Author: TESTYOUR Creator Published: November 11, 2026 Reading Time: ~7 min
"I want to become a doctor who treats sick kids from poor families and says: 'Don't worry about the fee—this one is on the house!'"

💼 A Quick Confession from the Site Creator

Across all the personality diagnostics and dossiers built on TESTYOUR, if you ever ask me to name a single undisputed favorite character in all of Hunter x Hunter, I will answer Leorio Paradinight without blinking.

(Keep your eyes peeled for a couple of Leorio Easter eggs tucked across this website! Today's essay is my love letter to why this stubborn, warm-hearted medical student outshines every prodigy in the series.)

Ask most anime fans to name their favorite character in Hunter x Hunter, and you will almost always hear the same list.

People gravitate toward Killua's lightning-fast assassination grace, Kurapika's tragic Scarlet Eyes, Hisoka's chaotic magnetism, Chrollo's poetic detachment, or Meruem's philosophical evolution. Standing beside those towering prodigies in a crumpled business suit, tiny round sunglasses, and a battered briefcase, Leorio often looks at first glance like comic relief—the loud older guy who complains about stairs and serves as a foil to the younger geniuses.

Truthfully, on my very first read-through years ago, I underestimated him too. Yet the older I get—and the more real-world bills, burnout, and unfair systems I watch friends navigate—the clearer a quiet revelation becomes: Leorio is the most genuinely heroic human being Yoshihiro Togashi ever wrote.

1. The "Money-Obsessed Cynic" Mask Over a Heartbreaking Promise

On the surface, Leorio enters the story making himself easy to dislike. While Gon seeks adventure and Kurapika fights for clan honor, Leorio brashly announces during the shipstorm interview that he wants the Hunter License purely for cash—even drawing a knife when Kurapika calls him shallow.

Then comes the confession that immediately strips away every cynical assumption we made about him:

"It was a curable disease... My best friend died simply because an operation cost an astronomical amount of money we didn't have!
So I decided to become a doctor. And when poor kids come in needing treatment, I want to look them in the eye and say, 'This one's free of charge!' Becoming a Hunter gets me into medical school and funds the bills. Laugh if you want!" — Hunter Exam Arc, Leorio revealing why he chases wealth

Think about the contrast among our four leads at the start of the story. Gon is driven by personal curiosity, Kurapika by vengeance, and Killua by boredom and a desperate wish to escape his family. Leorio is the only member of the quartet risking his life in a lethal exam purely so strangers won't die for lack of healthcare access.

And don't forget his canonical age: despite looking and shouting like an exhausted thirty-something salaryman, Leorio is nineteen years old. Being forced to grow up fast after watching a childhood friend die taught him early that noble ideals cannot buy surgical equipment—so he wore materialism like armor over a terribly tender conscience.

2. Quiet Grit and the Forgotten 8-Ton Testing Gate Feat

Because the 2011 anime adaptation streamlined several scenes at Kukuroo Mountain, many fans mistakenly remember Leorio as physically frail compared to Gon and Kurapika. In the original manga during the Zoldyck Family rescue arc, after completing twenty days of agonizing resistance training with Zebro, the three friends return to push open the monumental Testing Gate.

Each pair of doors doubles in weight starting at 4 tons. While Gon (recovering from his broken arm) and Kurapika pushed open the 1st Gate (4 tons total), Leorio planted his feet and pushed open the 2nd Gate—8 tons total—on his own. He had no magical bloodline and no assassin upbringing; he simply refused to let Killua stay locked away behind a door.

💼 True Strength Is Showing Up When Everyone Else Calculates the Odds

Back in the Numere Wetlands during Phase 1, completely collapsed from exhaustion, Leorio could have stayed safely hidden. Instead, the moment he heard Kurapika confront Hisoka, he ran straight back into lethal danger—ready to swing a wooden club at a mass-murdering Nen master just so his friend wouldn't face that terror alone. Later in Yorknew City, while Killua and Gon wrestled with Phantom Troupe bounties, it was Leorio driving the getaway car, running auctions, and keeping them anchored in reality.

Even when Leorio temporarily steps out of combat during Greed Island and the Chimera Ant crisis, Togashi treats his absence with dignity: he isn't sidelined because he became irrelevant—he is pulling brutal all-nighters studying for medical school entrance exams because he refuses to break a promise made years ago.

3. A Punch Forged Not to Kill, But to Heal: The Emission Hatsu Reveal

For every longtime Leorio fan, the pinnacle moment of Hunter x Hunter arrives during the 13th Hunter Chairman Election Arc.

Twelve-year-old Gon lies shriveled on life support, seconds from death, while his biological father Ging Freecss sits nonchalantly inside a political auditorium without once bothering to visit the hospital. As seasoned Zodiacs and hundred-star Hunters trade polite murmurs, Leorio steps up to the microphone, shakes with raw fury, and slams his fist onto the podium floor—launching an Emission warp punch that rips across the hall and decks Ging square in the jaw on live broadcast.

Watching a prodigy-obsessed absentee father take a clean left hook was satisfying enough. What turns that moment into pure writing magic is the private conversation afterward when Ging explains the real origin of Leorio's Hatsu to Cheadle:

  • Born in the Operating Room, Not the Battlefield: Ging immediately recognized that Leorio did not invent his portal shockwave to ambush duelists.
  • Ultrasound Palpation with Aura: Leorio practiced emitting fine waves of aura across solid surfaces like medical ultrasound sonar—allowing a surgeon to locate and break up inaccessible blood clots or inoperable internal tumors inside a patient without slicing them open.

Every fighter around him weaponized Nen to sever limbs, steal powers, or enslave minds. Leorio took the exact same life energy and asked: "How can I reach inside a human body to save someone a scalpel cannot touch?" That single detail sums up his entire soul.

4. Beating Political Chess with Unfiltered Humanity

Throughout the election, Pariston Hill played five-dimensional political chess against the entire Hunter Association. And yet, to Pariston's genuine amusement and alarm, an unranked 19-year-old med student rocketed straight to #2 in the final runoff.

Why did hardened Hunters vote overwhelmingly for Leorio? Not because he promised tax breaks or strategic patronage, but because in a hall full of schemers, he was the only candidate saying out loud what any decent human being felt: "Honestly, once Gon wakes up healthy, I don't even want this job—I just couldn't stand watching people sit around doing nothing!"

And when Killua and Alluka miraculously healed Gon and Gon walked through the auditorium doors, Leorio dropped his candidacy without hesitation, swept the boy into a tearful bear hug, and walked away proud—until Cheadle Yorkshire personally recruited him into the Zodiacs (replacing the Boar) to lead medical support aboard the Black Whale expedition to the Dark Continent.

Conclusion: Growing Up Means Seeing Why Leorio Wins

When we are young readers, tragedy and effortless genius feel glamorous. We want to be the lone wolf who never sweats or the brooding strategist who hides every tear.

Then real life hits. We realize how exhausting it is just to show up consistently for school, work, and the people we love. We see how rare it actually is to meet someone who refuses to look away when a friend is drowning, who refuses to sneer at ordinary effort, and who still carries an unashamed desire to heal people free of charge in a world obsessed with price tags.

Among gods, ants, and monsters, Leorio Paradinight stays fiercely, stubbornly human—and that is why he will always stand at the very top of my list.
