Scientific Background: Carl Jung's Cognitive Functions & 4-Axis Theory

1. Dynamic Multi-Dimensional Spectrum Beyond Stereotypes

Rooted in Swiss psychiatrist Carl Gustav Jung's Psychological Types and systematized by Katharine Briggs and Isabel Myers, the 16 Personalities framework evaluates how we direct energy, process information, evaluate decisions, and structure daily life. Rather than trapping people inside rigid binary labels, TESTYOUR measures your spectrum intensity across 30 varied scenarios with alternating pole orientations.

2. The Four Dichotomy Axes Explained

• Extraversion (E) vs. Introversion (I): Gaining momentum through external engagement versus recharging in reflective solitude.
• Sensing (S) vs. Intuition (N): Focusing on concrete facts and tactile verification versus conceptual patterns and future possibilities.
• Thinking (T) vs. Feeling (F): Prioritizing objective logic and fairness versus interpersonal harmony and empathetic impact.
• Judging (J) vs. Perceiving (P): Finding calm in clear structure and early completion versus adapting organically to open-ended options.
