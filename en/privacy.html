Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

At TESTYOUR UK (accessible at https://testyour.uk), protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) collected when you use our interactive assessments, static encyclopedias, and web utilities, as well as how cookies and third-party advertising networks operate on our site.

1. Zero Collection of Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

TESTYOUR does not require account registration, user sign-ins, names, street addresses, phone numbers, or personal email addresses to use any of our personality assessments or random draw tools.

All interactive quiz responses (in our 30-Question MBTI Test, Water Divination Nen Test, and Hunter Character Match) and all participant names entered into our Random Name Picker & Team Generator are processed 100% client-side in your browser's temporary memory (JavaScript RAM). Your quiz answers and entered names are never transmitted to, logged on, or stored in any backend database, and they are permanently cleared the moment you close or refresh your browser tab.

2. Cookies, LocalStorage, and Standard Web Log Files

Like most standard websites, our hosting servers automatically record non-identifying technical log entries when a page is requested. These logs may include anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, operating system, referring/exit URLs, and date/time stamps, which are used solely to maintain site security and diagnose broken links.

  • Browser LocalStorage: We use lightweight browser storage solely to remember non-personal UI preferences (such as dismissing the language suggestion banner).
  • Managing Cookies: You can configure your web browser settings at any time to block, delete, or alert you about cookies (e.g., in Chrome under Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies).

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie Disclosure

To keep our assessments and long-form essays free for all readers, TESTYOUR uses Google AdSense and certified third-party advertising partners to display advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to https://testyour.uk or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables Google and its partners to serve personalized or non-personalized ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com) or by reading the Google Advertising Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or www.youronlinechoices.eu.

4. GDPR (UK/EEA) and CCPA Data Protection Rights

Visitors residing in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area (EEA), or California have statutory rights under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and CCPA—including the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of personal data, and the right to opt out of personalized ad tracking. Because TESTYOUR does not collect or store personal user profiles on its servers, you may exercise full control over advertising cookies directly via your browser settings and Google's Ads Settings linked above.

5. Children's Privacy

TESTYOUR does not knowingly solicit or collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe any personal information has been inadvertently submitted to us via email inquiry, please contact us immediately so we can purge it.
