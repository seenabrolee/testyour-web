Four Psychological Axes Across the 8 Characters

• Instinctive Heart & Loyalty (Gon, Leorio): Driven by personal conviction and unbreakable loyalty—willing to sacrifice calculation to defend the people inside their circle.
• Calculated Precision & Control (Killua, Illumi): Prioritize survival probabilities, emotional detachment, and contingency exits before acting under danger.
• Principled Order & Systems (Kurapika, Chrollo): Master complex rules, vows, and group architectures to overcome structural disadvantages.
• Stimulus & Philosophical Mastery (Hisoka, Meruem): Seek formidable challenges, unexpected intellectual breakthroughs, and continuous self-evolution outside conventional norms.
