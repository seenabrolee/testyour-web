About TESTYOUR UK

Welcome to TESTYOUR UK (https://testyour.uk), an independent cultural and psychological web publication founded in 2026. Our mission is to bridge established analytical personality models with narrative subcultural archetypes, providing users with engaging tools for self-reflection and comparative character study.

1. Editorial Mission & Typology Philosophy

Understanding oneself is one of the most foundational human endeavors. We believe that personality typology—such as Carl Jung's psychological types (popularized in the 16-personality Myers-Briggs framework)—serves as a valuable heuristic for exploring cognitive diversity. By pairing these frameworks with rich fictional universes like Hunter x Hunter, we make complex psychological concepts accessible, entertaining, and educational.

2. Non-Commercial Fan-Made Disclaimer

All pop-culture references, names, and character concepts from Hunter x Hunter are the sole intellectual property of Yoshihiro Togashi, Shueisha, and associated license holders. TESTYOUR is an unofficial, non-commercial fan initiative operated under Fair Use principles for analytical and educational commentary. All character illustrations featured on this website are original digital fan-art interpretations and not official studio assets.

3. Transparency & Contact

We adhere to the highest standards of user privacy and transparency. We do not store personal identifiers, nor do we sell user assessment data to third parties. For questions, suggestions, or editorial corrections, please reach out to our team at contact@testyour.uk.
