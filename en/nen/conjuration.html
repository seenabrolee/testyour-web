Conjuration (具現化系)

Materialization

Conjurers can create physical, independent material objects out of pure aura. Unlike simple aura shapes, conjured items have mass, can be touched by normal humans, and can carry supernatural vows.

1. Water Divination Physical Reaction (水見式)

Glass & Leaf Observable Phenomenon

Impurities, crystals, or small particles materialize inside the water.

When a Conjurer places both hands around the divination glass, visible solid impurities, mineral crystals, or sediment spontaneously materialize inside the clear water. Unlike Transmutation, which only changes aura's properties, Conjuration condenses pure life energy into tangible, physical matter that even non-Nen users can see and touch.

2. Hisoka's Aura Personality Diagnosis

In Hisoka's psychological framework, the mental discipline required to visualize and materialize every atomic detail of an object belongs to high-strung, hyper-analytical perfectionists:

🃏 Hisoka's Diagnosis: "High-Strung Perfectionist"

"Sharp, jagged crystals materializing within crystal-clear water... such high-strung, fastidious aura♦

An uncompromising perfectionist—Conjuration♣
Calculating thousands of contingencies just to materialize an imagined object into reality.

Paranoid and exhausting to deal with, perhaps... but that chilling obsessiveness to stake your very life on strict vows and conditions just to forge lethal power is dreadfully alluring♥
Just remember: the moment you trap yourself inside your own rules, you're finished♠ There's nothing more boring than a puppeteer tangled in their own strings."

Under a psychological lens, Conjurers exhibit intense analytical vigilance combined with strict internal standards. Before committing to action, they simulate worst-case failure modes and build contingency rules—explaining why their sharpest combat power often grows out of voluntary conditions and severe vows. In real life, this conscientious discipline makes them extraordinarily reliable architects and investigators, provided they guard against obsessive rigidity and stress-induced burnout.

Aura Psychological Profile

High-strung, fastidious, stoic, cautious, and intensely analytical.

3. Combat Mechanics & Tactical Capabilities

Materializes physical objects from aura with supernatural rules, conditions, and oaths.

  • Objects can be seen and touched by ordinary non-Nen users, enabling tactical stealth
  • Can imbue created items with supernatural conditional abilities (e.g., Chain Jail, Blinky)
  • Meticulous combat planning leaves almost zero procedural vulnerability
  • High compatibility with Transmutation (80%) and Specialization (potential late bloomer)

4. Strategic Vulnerabilities & Counters

  • Lowest natural physical reinforcement (60% Enhancement), making melee trades dangerous
  • Overly strict vows and conditions can cause fatal backfire if broken
  • Extremely high visualization and mental training threshold to conjure even a single object

5. Iconic Masters of this Affinity

Renowned Nen Masters

Kurapika, Shizuku Murasaki, Kite, Kortopi, Genthru
