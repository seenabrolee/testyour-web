Manipulation (操作系)

Control & Puppetry

Manipulators pour aura into living creatures or objects to control their movements and minds. Fulfilling specific conditions can lead to an absolute, unblockable mental override.

1. Water Divination Physical Reaction (水見式)

Glass & Leaf Observable Phenomenon

The leaf or floating object on top of the water moves across the surface.

Water Divination is the universal Shingen-ryu Nen evaluation protocol. By immersing one's hands around a glass filled to the brim with water and a floating leaf, the pupil projects Ren aura to induce an observable physical transformation.
